# QRK copy ideas

Brainstorm from September 10, 2026. These are candidates, not final brand copy.

## Direction

QRK is a personal site builder in the vein of Linktree. Encourage people to
emphasize their quirks, flaunt what they've got, and build something unmistakably
their own. The tone is playful permission: show off, be particular, and take up
space.

## Headline candidates

- **Be a little much.** — The strongest initial direction: permission to show off.
- **You do you. Make a page about it.** — Playful and immediately understandable.
- **All your quirks. On full display.** — Strongest connection to the QRK name.
- **Make yourself hard to scroll past.** — Confident and a little cheeky.
- **Big you energy.** — Short and loud.
- **A little page. A lot of you.** — Warm and memorable.
- **Give your weird a home.** — More niche, more personality.
- **An internet with more you in it.** — Inviting and optimistic.
- **Go ahead. Make it personal.** — A headline or CTA.

## Hero candidates

### Be a little much.

Your projects, obsessions, side quests, and shameless self-promotion. Build a page
with your personality all over it.

CTA: **Make it yours**

### You do you. Make a page about it.

Bring everything you make, love, and want to show off together on a site that's
unmistakably yours.

CTA: **Build your QRK**

## Competitor references

Homepage copy observed September 10, 2026; these are reference points, not QRK copy.

| Site | Copy | Positioning read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/) | “A website makes it real” | Legitimacy and ambition. |
| [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/) | “A link in bio built for you.” | Usefulness and personalization. |
| [own.page](https://own.page/) | “Make your page feel like you” | Self-expression, closest to QRK's proposed direction. |
